## 1. Project Overview
Project AAROHAN is an enterprise-grade **Living Digital Banking Twin and alternative MSME underwriting sandbox**. It replicates India’s Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) stack to automate credit assessments, reduce underwriting times, and stress-test loan portfolios under macroeconomic parameters.

---

## 2. Vision Statement
To bridge India’s ₹20+ Trillion MSME credit gap by democratizing automated cash-flow-based underwriting, enabling banks to issue credit securely in under five minutes.

---

## 3. Problem Statement
Commercial MSME underwriting is hindered by manual checks, siloed tax/payroll records, and high integration costs. Financial institutions lack low-risk, compliant environments to simulate credit policies, resulting in slow product cycles and credit risk exposures.

---

## 4. Market Opportunity
The shift to cash-flow-based lending presents a massive opportunity. Rising adoption of OCEN and Account Aggregator stacks allows commercial banks and FinTechs to tap into previously underserved micro-merchant segments.

---

## 5. Solution Overview
AAROHAN delivers a complete **Enterprise Simulation Engine (ESE)** sandbox. It emulates external public registries (GSTN, EPFO, CKYC, MCA) locally, permitting instant, end-to-end loan journeys with 100% test coverage and zero production boundary leaks.

---

## 6. Key Innovations
- **Financial Causal Engine**: Links changes in repo rates, inflation, and seasonal rainfall directly to client ledger accounts.
- **Presenter Demo Console**: Offers guided timelines, tick step-throughs, and narrations.
- **Dynamic White-Labeling**: Swaps layouts and colors (STANDARD, IDBI Bank, and HACKATHON neon) on-the-fly.

---

## 7. Enterprise Digital Banking Twin Highlights
Replicates both borrowers (5 industry sectors, 6 states) and public institutions, enabling high-fidelity testing of compliance checks and API limits.

---

## 8. AI & Enterprise Simulation Engine
Calculates FHC (Financial Health Card) metrics and underwriting decisions. Decision logic is deterministic, ensuring repeatable demonstration paths.

---

## 9. Business Benefits
- **Accelerated GTM**: Reduces digital lending integration cycles from months to days.
- **Lower Training Costs**: Interactive timelines facilitate staff training.
- **Proactive Risk Mitigation**: Simulates regional economic stress before credit exposure.

---

## 10. Customer Value Proposition
Ensures small business loans are approved and disbursed in under 5 minutes without physical collateral requirements.

---

## 11. Technology Highlights
Built on Python FastAPI, SQLite (in WAL mode for high concurrency), Docker containers, and Google Cloud Run. Decoupled adapters switch between DEMO and PRODUCTION profiles dynamically.

---

## 12. Competitive Differentiators
Traditional sandboxes use static, non-isolated mock servers. AAROHAN models transactional ledgers, regional monsoons, and event cascades, backed by 100% test coverage.

---

## 13. Commercialization Strategy
Distributed via direct sales, Google Cloud Marketplace, and system integrator networks. 

---

## 14. Deployment Options
- **SaaS / Managed Cloud**: Multi-tenant deployments on Google Cloud Run.
- **On-Premise VPC**: Dockerized deployments inside secure banking environments.
