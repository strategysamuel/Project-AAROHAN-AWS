---

## Strategic Context & Vision 2035
*   **Vision 2035:** Establish IDBI Bank as a leading provider of digital cash-flow credit to micro and small businesses. This is achieved by using automated underwriting engines and national DPI integrations to replace collateral-based credit evaluation models.
*   **Strategic Imperatives:** Address competitive pressures from agile fintech lenders and satisfy priority sector lending sub-targets while maintaining strict risk standards.

---

## Business, Financial & AI Transformation Cases
*   **Business Case:** Automated ingestion of GST invoices and bank statements (via AA) reduces file collection times, allowing relationship managers to handle more accounts.
*   **Financial Case:** The project targets a payback period of under 3 years, driven by a projected 15% reduction in the operational cost-to-income ratio and a 35% increase in New-to-Credit (NTC) MSME loan volumes.
*   **AI Case:** Vertex AI and Gemini models automate document spreading and draft credit memos, shifting underwriter roles to exception review and validation.

---

## Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) & Ecosystem Alignment
*   **AA & GSTN:** Automates borrower cash-flow analysis by connecting directly to Account Aggregator and GST networks.
*   **ONDC, ULI & OCEN:** Integrates with national digital networks to enable point-of-sale financing at low customer acquisition costs.

---

## Google Cloud Strategic Partnership Value
*   **Security & Scale:** Zero-Trust connections, Cloud Armor web firewalls, and Cloud KMS key management keep data secure.
*   **AI Innovation:** Native Vertex AI Agent Engine and ADK frameworks simplify multi-agent coordination without requiring complex middleware integrations.

## Executive Board Scorecards
The platform monitors and reports performance across C-level scorecards:

### Board of Directors Scorecard
*   **Metrics:** Return on Assets (ROA), Cost-to-Income Ratio, priority sector lending compliance, Gross NPA.
*   **Target Baseline:** ROA > 1.5%; Cost-to-Income -15% reduction; Gross NPA < 1.5%.

### Chief Credit Officer (CCO) Scorecard
*   **Metrics:** Average credit file underwriting TAT, CAM compilation backlog, policy exception override rate.
*   **Target Baseline:** Underwriting TAT < 30 minutes; policy exceptions < 5%.

## Executive Risk Register & Board Decision Options

The Executive Risk Register summarizes primary implementation risks:

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Severity | Mitigation Strategy | GCP Service Mapping |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **RSK-001** | Model Performance Drift | High | Deploy continuous bias and drift validation sweeps. | Vertex AI Monitor |
| **RSK-002** | Registry API Outages | Medium | Implement queue retry workflows and manual portals. | Cloud Workflows |
| **RSK-003** | Data Privacy Violations | High | Enforce automated PII masking and IAM access roles. | Secret Manager, KMS |
| **RSK-004** | Operational Adoption | Medium | Run branch training pilots and sandbox exercises. | Looker tracking |

### Board Decision Options
1.  **Option A (Recommended):** Approve the full Project AAROHAN investment budget and launch the phased pilot branches rollout.
2.  **Option B:** Defer the launch to execute additional validation trials in sandbox environments.
3.  **Option C:** Cancel the transformation and retain traditional paper-based underwriting workflows.
