# Demo Script 5 Minutes

## 0:00 to 0:30

Open the live portal and sign in with the demo relationship manager account. State that the platform is the customer-facing MSME lending twin.

## 0:30 to 1:15

Show the dashboard and explain that the portal is role-aware and built for a banker’s workflow. Point out the active persona and the summary metrics.

## 1:15 to 2:15

Open Demo Studio and explain that the platform can replay a guided lending journey with seeded personas. Mention that the workflow is deterministic for judging.

## 2:15 to 3:15

Step through Customer Onboarding, CKYC, GST, AA, EPFO, and MCA. Emphasize that the platform consolidates fragmented checks into one view.

## 3:15 to 4:15

Open Financial Health Card, Credit Decision, and CAM. Explain that the outputs are explainable and designed for underwriting review.

## 4:15 to 5:00

Open Executive Dashboard, Reports, and AI Banking Copilot. Close by stating that the platform turns a complex MSME lending process into a guided, auditable, judge-friendly workflow.
