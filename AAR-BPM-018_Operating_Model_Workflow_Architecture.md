## Executive Summary
This document defines the Enterprise Banking Operating Model & Intelligent Workflow Architecture (AAR-BPM-018) for Project AAROHAN. It maps the business operational structures, agent collaboration handoffs, event routing strategies, decision matrixes, and workflow SLA profiles. The ERDA guidelines ensure that all workflows align with native Google Cloud services (such as Cloud Workflows, Eventarc, Pub/Sub, and Vertex AI Agent Engine) to support automated cash-flow lending operations.

---

## Operating Model Vision & Principles
*   **Vision:** Build an agile, event-driven banking operations model that transitions staff from manual processing to exception management and client advisory.
*   **Operating Principles:** Zero Manual Intervention (target), Continuous Risk Monitoring, Loose-coupled Event Orchestration, and Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards.

---

## Business & AI Operating Models
*   **Underwriting Desk:** Receives pre-compiled Credit Assessment Memos (CAM) generated by the AI Credit Agent. Underwriters act as exception managers.
*   **Relationship Management Desk:** Receives next-best-action alerts and pre-drafted client emails generated by the AI RM Agent.
*   **Risk Operations Desk:** Receives continuous EWS alerts generated by the AI Risk Agent monitoring client transaction trends.

## Intelligent Workflow Architecture

Below is the design specification for key workflows:

### Workflow 1: Customer Onboarding & Verification
*   **Workflow ID:** WF-001
*   **Purpose:** Register and verify customer identity profiles using national registries.
*   **Actors:** Borrower, RM, Operations Team.
*   **AI Agents:** Document Agent, Customer Agent.
*   **Inputs:** PAN, Udyam certificate, CKYC ID, mobile verification key.
*   **Outputs:** Verified customer profile, active customer ID.
*   **Business Rules:** Applicants must hold active Udyam certificates and GST registrations.
*   **Decision Gates:** Registration validity checks, AML screening clearance.
*   **Human Approvals:** Operations analyst signs off on AML exception alerts.
*   **AI Recommendations:** Document Agent verifies uploaded certificate authenticity.
*   **SLAs:** Onboarding workflow completed in < 15 minutes.
*   **Escalations:** Escalates to Branch Manager queue if unresolved in 2 hours.
*   **Notifications:** Send SMS/Email updates to the borrower.
*   **Audit Events:** Customer Registration Started, Identity Verified, Onboarding Completed.
*   **Compliance Checks:** Direct PAN and CKYC registry validation.
*   **Exception Handling:** Route mismatched registrations to manual correction queues.
*   **KPIs:** Onboarding TAT, completion rate.
*   **Future Google Cloud Mapping:** Cloud Workflows, Firebase, Eventarc, Apigee, Document AI.

---

### Workflow 2: Alternate Data Spreading & Health Assessment
*   **Workflow ID:** WF-002
*   **Purpose:** Automatically retrieve and normalise bank statements and tax logs (GSTN, AA).
*   **Actors:** Credit Officer, Underwriter, RM.
*   **AI Agents:** Credit Agent, Document Agent.
*   **Inputs:** Valid consent token, tax ID (GSTIN), bank statement files.
*   **Outputs:** Normalized cash flow spreads, Financial Health Card score.
*   **Business Rules:** limits calculation must use the Nayak turnover method.
*   **Decision Gates:** Consent check validation, bank statement integrity verification.
*   **Human Approvals:** Underwriter signs off on ratio policy overrides.
*   **AI Recommendations:** Credit Agent flags cash flow anomalies and hidden EMIs.
*   **SLAs:** Spreading and health card generation completed in < 5 minutes.
*   **Escalations:** Escalates to Credit Head queue if unresolved in 1 hour.
*   **Notifications:** Send progress updates to RMs.
*   **Audit Events:** Alternate Data Pull Initiated, Data Received, Spreading Completed.
*   **Compliance Checks:** RBI digital lending guidelines and consent checks.
*   **Exception Handling:** Route files with missing tax fields to manual upload queues.
*   **KPIs:** Spreading TAT, calculation accuracy.
*   **Future Google Cloud Mapping:** Cloud Workflows, AlloyDB, BigQuery, Vertex AI, Pub/Sub.

## AI Agent Collaboration & Orchestration

Specialized agents coordinate tasks using event-driven handoffs:

1.  **RM Agent** identifies client lead -> triggers **Customer Agent** to start onboarding.
2.  Onboarding finishes -> **Customer Agent** requests consent -> **Credit Agent** pulls GSTN/AA files.
3.  **Credit Agent** compiles spreads -> **Risk Agent** checks limits -> routes file to underwriter queue.
4.  **Portfolio Agent** checks ongoing transaction logs -> flags EWS alert -> notifies **RM Agent** to review.

## Enterprise Event Architecture
The platform runs on Eventarc and Pub/Sub routing channels, capturing 16 core events:
*   *Customer Created:* Triggers identity checks.
*   *Consent Granted:* Triggers data fetch pipelines (GSTN/AA).
*   *Financial Health Updated:* Triggers limit score calculations.
*   *Loan Disbursed:* Updates core bank ledgers.
*   *EWS Triggered:* Dispatches alerts to the RM console.

## Decision & SLA Frameworks
*   **Decision Categories:** Human (final sanction, override approval), AI (limit scoring recommendations, EWS anomaly predictions), Hybrid (pre-compiled CAM review).
*   **SLA Scorecard:** Customer Onboarding (< 15 mins) | Credit Assessment (< 30 mins) | Approval TAT (< 2 hours) | Notifications (< 5 seconds).
