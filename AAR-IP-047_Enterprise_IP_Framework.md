## 1. Executive Summary & Strategy Vision

### Executive Summary
Project AAROHAN represents a critical operational platform and a valuable proprietary asset for IDBI Bank. This framework outlines our strategy to identify, document, protect, and extract value from the innovations built during the project. By establishing guidelines for patents, trade secrets, software reuse, and strategic partnership models, this framework secures our technology advantages while preparing the platform to support future business lines and digital partner networks.

### Innovation Asset Vision
IDBI Bank treats its custom-developed AI agents, proprietary risk scoring algorithms, and orchestration models as high-value intellectual property. We aim to secure our market position by keeping proprietary decision logics protected as trade secrets, filing patents for novel software architectures, and reusing modular components to lower development costs for other business lines.

### Platform Ownership Principles
1. **Clear Division of IP:** IDBI Bank owns all custom software configurations, prompt designs, data pipelines, and database schemas developed for AAROHAN.
2. **Respect for Open Standards:** Use open-source tools (like Model Context Protocol) and vendor services (like Google Cloud APIs) without claiming ownership of the underlying public frameworks.
3. **Traceable Contributions:** All code, documentation, and design assets must be linked to specific development runs to verify creation rights.

### Innovation Protection Principles
*   **Identify Early:** Review software releases monthly to flag patentable architectures or trade secrets.
*   **Secure Access:** Protect proprietary algorithms by using strict access controls and encrypted code repositories.
*   **Verify Compliance:** Ensure all custom components comply with banking regulations and data safety guidelines.

## 3. Platform Commercialization Strategic Options

To maximize the value of Project AAROHAN, IDBI Bank has several strategic options to reuse and scale the platform without compromising customer data privacy or regulatory compliance:

```
                  [ COMMERCIALIZATION OPTIONS ]
                               │
         ┌─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┐
         ▼                     ▼                     ▼
  [ Internal Reuse ]    [ Ecosystem Lending ]   [ Platform Banking ]
  - Retail Underwriting - Co-Lending NBFCs       - Third-Party APIs
  - Corporate Risk      - Supply Chain Networks  - Merchant Portals
  - Subsidiary Systems  - Trade Hubs             - Partner Portals
```

*   **Internal Enterprise Reuse:** Deploy the underwriting, early warning, and document processing components across other banking business lines (such as retail credit, agricultural loans, or corporate credit).

*   **Banking-as-a-Platform (BaaP):** Offer secure credit verification and scoring APIs to third-party merchant apps, allowing them to embed credit options into their services.

## 4. Innovation Governance & Review Pipeline

```
  [ DEVELOPMENT ] ──> [ IP DISCOVERY ] ──> [ ASSET REVIEW ] ──> [ CLASSIFICATION ] ──> [ PROTECTION ]
  - Dev Teams         - Release Audits    - Innovation Board  - Patent / Trade Secret - Code Encryption
  - Tech Partners     - Monthly checks    - Monthly meetings  - Reuse Evaluation     - Patent Filings
```

1.  **Innovation Review Board (IRB):** Meets monthly to review new software updates, identify valuable assets, and check compliance parameters.
2.  **IP Discovery Process:** Development leads submit release notes to the IRB to flag patentable architectures or proprietary methods.
3.  **Asset Classification:** The IRB determines whether to file a patent application, catalog the asset as a trade secret, or release it internally as a reusable software component.
4.  **Knowledge Protection:** Enforce secure coding guidelines, restrict code access to authorized developers, and use encrypted repositories.
