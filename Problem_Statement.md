# Problem Statement

MSME lending is slowed by fragmented data checks, inconsistent underwriting workflows, and the difficulty of presenting a clear decision story to business reviewers. A banker must often gather information from identity, business registry, tax, bank statement, workforce, and internal risk systems before a loan can be approved or rejected.

This fragmentation creates three recurring issues:

- Long turnaround time for credit decisions.
- Manual, inconsistent, and hard-to-audit underwriting reviews.
- Poor visibility for executives and relationship managers who need a concise view of portfolio health and borrower risk.

Project AAROHAN addresses this by turning the lending process into a single guided experience with explainable outputs, role-based access, and demo-ready data paths. The goal is not to replace a bank’s systems, but to unify the journey so it can be understood, tested, and presented quickly.

## Desired Outcome

A judge or banker should be able to:

- Log in securely.
- Walk a borrower through onboarding and registry checks.
- Review financial health, credit risk, and CAM outputs.
- Inspect executive dashboards and reports.
- Observe a coherent, auditable lending narrative without manual stitching across systems.
