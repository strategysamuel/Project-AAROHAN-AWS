# AI Features

Project AAROHAN uses AI to make the lending workflow easier to explain, faster to navigate, and more useful for relationship managers and executives.

## AI Capabilities

- Role-aware AI Banking Copilot.
- Suggested prompts and quick actions based on user role and context.
- Recommended reports linked to active workflow state.
- Explainable credit and financial health outputs that support human review.

## Live Copilot Experience

The live portal shows the copilot as a banking assistant configured for the current authenticated role. In the verified browser session, the copilot exposed:

- Suggested prompts for onboarding, CAM generation, borrower comparison, and underwriting.
- Quick commands for GST compliance, financial health, and customer profile actions.
- Report recommendations aligned to the current lending journey.

## Value of the AI Layer

The AI layer is intentionally assistive rather than autonomous. Its purpose is to help the user navigate the workflow, summarize outputs, and surface the right next action, not to replace lending governance or approval controls.

## Key Benefits

- Reduces navigation friction.
- Supports explainable decision review.
- Adapts guidance to the user’s role.
- Fits the judge demo by turning complex workflow state into concise prompts.
