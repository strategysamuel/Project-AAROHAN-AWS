# Project AAROHAN Demo Script

## Presentation Length

Target runtime: 5 to 7 minutes

## Opening

### 0:00 to 0:35 - Welcome and context

Script:

"Project AAROHAN is an enterprise MSME lending platform and digital banking twin. In the next few minutes, I will show how a borrower moves from login to an informed credit decision, how the platform generates a CAM, and how the executive team sees the portfolio outcome."

Speaker notes:

- Keep the introduction short and confident.
- Mention that the journey is deterministic so judges can replay it consistently.

## Step 1 - Login

### 0:35 to 1:10 - Authenticate into the portal

Script:

"I will sign in with a seeded demo account. The portal uses the existing authentication service and routes me into the dashboard immediately after login."

Speaker notes:

- Use the default Relationship Manager account.
- Pause briefly after login so the dashboard state is visible.

## Step 2 - Demo Persona

### 1:10 to 1:50 - Select the borrower profile

Script:

"Next, I open the RC1 Demo Studio and choose a curated persona. This lets us demonstrate different lending outcomes without resetting the environment manually."

Speaker notes:

- Call out the persona name clearly.
- If needed, note whether the borrower is healthy, watchlist, or high risk.

## Step 3 - Lending Journey

### 1:50 to 3:20 - Walk through the underwriting flow

Script:

"Now the journey moves through onboarding and the registry checks: identity verification, CKYC, GST, Account Aggregator, EPFO, and MCA. Each service contributes to a structured credit file instead of forcing the reviewer to collect information from separate systems."

Speaker notes:

- Emphasize that the same journey can be replayed for multiple personas.
- Use the guided walkthrough to keep the presentation focused.

## Step 4 - AI Decision

### 3:20 to 4:10 - Show the credit result

Script:

"The platform then synthesizes the financial health view and presents the credit decision in an explainable format. Rather than just showing an approval or rejection, it explains the core signals behind the outcome."

Speaker notes:

- Highlight the Financial Health Card and the decision summary.
- Mention that the demo is deterministic, which is helpful for judging.

## Step 5 - Marketplace

### 4:10 to 4:50 - Demonstrate embedded lending or OCEN-style flow

Script:

"Once the borrower is assessed, the platform can route the case into marketplace-style or OCEN-style flows. This demonstrates how the lending decision can connect to an embedded distribution model."

Speaker notes:

- Keep this section concise.
- Use it to explain how the platform extends beyond a single internal workflow.

## Step 6 - CAM

### 4:50 to 5:35 - Generate the CAM

Script:

"The next output is the CAM. This gives the reviewer a structured appraisal memo that captures the borrower profile, the key risk signals, and the recommendation in a format a credit team can work with."

Speaker notes:

- Pause on the report download or preview.
- Make it clear that this is a review artifact, not just a screen mockup.

## Step 7 - Executive Dashboard

### 5:35 to 6:30 - Close with portfolio visibility

Script:

"Finally, the executive dashboard shows the business value of the platform: active pipeline, approval rates, portfolio value, and early warning indicators. The story is no longer just about a single borrower; it becomes a portfolio and operating-model view."

Speaker notes:

- Point out the KPI tiles and the recent activity feed.
- End by showing that the same platform supports both front-line users and executives.

## Closing

### 6:30 to 7:00 - Final message

Script:

"Project AAROHAN turns MSME underwriting into a guided enterprise experience. It is designed to be understandable in minutes, repeatable in a hackathon setting, and extensible for a real lending platform. Thank you."

Speaker notes:

- End on the business value, not the implementation details.
- If time remains, invite questions about the architecture or demo personas.
